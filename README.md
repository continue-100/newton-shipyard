# Shipyard

The canonical list of current, ongoing student-led initiatives at our university. Aimed at allowing students to take ideas developed over a Hackathon and continue developing these as sustainable, publicly-contributed software.

## The Why

As mentioned above, there's a significant quantity of high-quality software produced each year by our undergraduates. However, ~90% will see no further life than their respective Hackathon and sit dormant on some random undergraduate's Github page. In parallel, new undergraduates (and others) wishing to begin contributing to meaningful campus software have no means to identify what is currently running and actively managed by our community.

Shipyard is less a technical problem and more a campus sociology experiment built around a few core observations:

- ### Public visibility incentivizes sustainability

  By hosting this list centrally, we can encourage project owners to continue supporting and making their services accessible beyond a single Hack.

- ### Building legacy within the NST S-VYASA campus

  Few graduating seniors will wish their hard-earned projects to cease operation. Equally, lower-year undergrads will look to find suitable repositories to begin working on. Showing the history and flow of projects through successive cohorts should motivate continued support.

- ### Facilitate initial contribution

  Providing a simple list of active initiatives will meet the needs of users, but not enable students to make their first pull requests. Including e.g. Good First Issues, languages used, and email(s) of point-of-contact people should significantly increase the number of prospective contributors. This will be particularly useful to promote to 1st years, as well as ahead of Hacktoberfest and GSoC.

- ### Drive verifiable activity

  Any manually-populated list will quickly fall out of date as students graduate. We aim to implement a series of automated processes to ensure the health of each listing, e.g. noting projects who are 'Seeking Maintainers'.

## Current Listings

Projects will be listed under one of three headings:

- Active: Recent activity and maintainers available to respond.
- Maintenance: Currently operational and willing to accept patches.
- Seeking Maintainer: Still running and would welcome one or more students to continue the work.
